## POST 2: "The 5 Questions"

**Hook**
The first 60 seconds of a booking call decide whether you get a 1-time groom or a 3-year client.

**Body**

The first 60 seconds of a booking call decide whether you get a 1-time groom
or a 3-year client.

Most groomers ask: "What day works?"

Great groomers ask these 5 things first. Free, copy it into your notes app:

1. **Pet name + breed.** Use the name for the rest of the call. Owners notice.
2. **Size and coat.** "Is Bailey's coat short, or long and curly?" This sets the time slot *and* the price.
3. **Last groom.** "When was Bailey last groomed?" Over 8 weeks means matting risk. Tell them the price *before* they're surprised.
4. **Behavior.** "Anything Bailey hates? Dryer, nail trims, being touched near the ears?" Now you look like a pro and you can plan the groom.
5. **Vaccines.** "Are Bailey's shots up to date?" Ask once, up front. Cleaner than awkward conversations at the door.

Then, and only then: "Great, I have Thursday at 10 or Friday at 2."

Why this works:
- The owner feels heard, so price becomes a smaller part of the decision.
- You walk into the groom already prepared.
- Every answer is a note for next time ("hates the dryer") that makes the second visit feel personal.

Try it on your next 5 calls. You'll feel the difference.

Bonus: quote a price *range* with the reason ("$85 to $110 depending on
coat condition"), not a single number. It stops the "you said $70" argument at
the door.

## POST 3: "The 6-Week Clock"

**Hook**
Your best customers are already on your calendar. They just don't know it yet.

**Body**

Your best customers are already on your calendar. They just don't know it yet.

Most dogs need grooming every 4 to 8 weeks depending on coat.
So every client you groom today is a booking you *could* have in 6 weeks.

Most groomers leave that to chance. Here's the free system:

**At pickup (10 seconds):**
"Bailey's due in about 6 weeks. Want me to hold Thursday the 14th at 10?"
Ask *before* they walk away. A specific date beats "call me when you need me."

**Day 35 (text, copy this):**
"Hi Sarah, it's [Name]. Bailey's coming up on 6 weeks and the coat's probably
starting to tangle. I have Thurs 10am or Fri 2pm open. Want one?"

**Day 45 (only if no reply):**
"No rush! Just don't want Bailey matting up. Reply YES and I'll grab you a slot."

That's it. Two texts and one question at pickup.

What this does:
- Turns one-off grooms into a recurring schedule
- Fills the calendar before it's empty
- Keeps Bailey's coat healthier, which owners love you for

The whole thing dies without records. If the last-groom date lives in your head
or a sticky note, you'll forget half your clients by week 5.

Steal the texts. Set 2 phone reminders per client. Try it for 30 days.
