Common questions

What kind of calls does the AI Receptionist handle for a shop?

"Are you open," "do you have X in stock," "where are you located" — the questions that otherwise interrupt whoever is working the counter. It answers instantly and logs the contact in your CRM.

Do I need an online store?

Often not. For most local shops the priority is getting someone through the door — hours, directions, stock and a phone number that always gets answered. A catalog or cart is available as part of the Custom Website tier if you are ready to sell online.

What matters most for a local shop?

Your Google Business Profile, included with the Custom Website tier. Most "near me" searches never reach a website at all — they end at the map result.

Can you show products without a full catalog?

Yes. A simple browsable product or menu section covers most shops on the Custom Website tier, and it is far cheaper to maintain than a full e-commerce build.
