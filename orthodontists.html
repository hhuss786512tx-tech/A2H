Questions

Does it work with my practice software?

Not directly. It does not connect to Ortho2, Cloud 9, OrthoTrac or Dentrix today. Every call lands in the CRM with a summary your front desk can add to your software in seconds.

Is this HIPAA-safe?

It handles inquiries, not clinical data. Calls collect a name, the reason for the call and callback details. It should not be used to collect health or payment-card information. Anything that touches PHI belongs in your existing compliant software.

Will parents know it is AI?

If they ask, it tells them. You choose the greeting.
