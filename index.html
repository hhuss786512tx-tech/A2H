Who is A2H?

A2H is an AI consulting studio in Houston, Texas, founded by Abbas Hussain. We build websites with an AI chat and a CRM lead list for small Texas trade businesses, mainly foundation repair, water damage restoration and garage door companies. Abbas Hussain is an accepted developer on AI Frontrunners.

How much does a website cost?

$800 to $2,000 one time, depending on the number of pages and features. Every build includes the AI chat and the CRM lead list. Hosting, upkeep and add-ons are quoted on the call.

Do I see the site before I pay?

Yes. We build a free sample site with your company name, trade and service area, and walk you through it on a 15-minute call. If you don't want it, you don't pay.

What does the AI chat do?

It answers common questions using the information you give us, takes the visitor's name, number and what they need, and saves it to your CRM marked by urgency. Anything it can't answer goes to you.

Do you only work with foundation, restoration and garage door companies?

Those are our focus, but we also build for other local businesses. Past work includes healthcare consulting, medical billing, biotech research and construction.

Do you offer an AI phone receptionist?

Yes, as an add-on. It answers calls when you can't, takes the caller's details and logs them to the same CRM. Pricing is quoted on the call.

Want to see your sample site?

We build it free, show it to you in 15 minutes, and you decide. No pressure, no obligation.
