Common questions

What does A2H do for a home care agency?

A website with an AI chat that answers the questions families ask (service area, types of help, how to start), takes the family's details and saves them to a CRM your office works each morning. The AI phone receptionist add-on does the same for calls you miss.

Does it manage caregiver scheduling or call-outs?

No. It handles inquiries only. It does not manage caregiver schedules, shift coverage or call-outs.

Is this for non-medical or medical home care?

A2H builds for non-medical home care agencies. The chat and CRM handle inquiry intake only, never clinical records, and do not give clinical advice.

Do I have to change my business number?

No. Your number stays the same and goes at the top of the site. If you add the phone receptionist, your existing line forwards to it when it is busy or unanswered. No porting.

What happens when a family inquiry needs a real person?

The chat passes anything it cannot answer to your office, with the person's details. The phone receptionist add-on can transfer the call to your office, or take a message and flag the contact in the CRM.

Where does A2H work?

A2H builds remotely for Texas businesses, including those around Houston, Dallas, Austin, San Antonio and Fort Worth.

What does it cost?

A website with the AI chat and CRM lead list is $800 to $2,000 one time, depending on size. You see a free sample site first. The AI phone receptionist is an add-on, quoted on the call, as are hosting and upkeep.
