# Photo shot list — Global Food Express

Every image on the site is currently a **labeled placeholder** generated by
`scripts/make-placeholders.mjs`. Replace each file in `public/images/` with a
real photograph of the same filename and aspect ratio; nothing else needs to
change. Shoot in landscape or portrait as listed, at least the pixel size shown
(bigger is fine, Next.js resizes).

General direction: warm, low light, shallow depth of field, real product with
brand names legible, no stock-photo gloss, no people unless noted (get written
consent for staff). Avoid AI-generated imagery; if any is used it must be
flagged for client approval.

Priority order: 1 hero, 2 storefronts, 3 butcher counter, 4 spice wall,
5 produce, 6 the rest.

## Video (optional, big upgrade)

10 to 15 seconds, 1080p, slow handheld or gimbal: spices scooped from a sack,
a grill or tawa sizzling, produce being misted, the butcher trimming. Export as
H.264 MP4 under 3 MB (`ffmpeg -i in.mov -vf scale=1600:-2 -an -crf 30 -preset slow out.mp4`)
plus a poster JPG. Then set `heroVideo` in `src/config/site.ts`.

## Alt text

Alt text for each image lives in `src/config/images.ts`. When a real photo
replaces a placeholder, update the `alt` to describe what is actually in frame.
