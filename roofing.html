Common questions

Can it handle a flood of messages after a storm?

Yes. Every message is answered and logged in the order it arrived, with spam filtered out. Anything the chat cannot answer is passed to you with the homeowner's details.

Does it work with insurance-claim roofing leads?

It captures the homeowner's name, what happened and a callback number. How you handle the claim after that is up to you.

How much does a website cost for a roofing company?

A2H builds are $800 to $2,000 one time, depending on the number of pages and features, including the AI chat and the CRM lead list. Hosting and upkeep are quoted on the call. You see a free sample site first.

Do you offer an AI phone receptionist?

Yes, as an add-on to a website build. It answers calls when you cannot, takes the caller's name, number and what they need, and logs it to the same CRM. Pricing is quoted on the call.

Do I have to change my business number?

No. Your number stays the same and goes at the top of the site, tappable on a phone. If you add the phone receptionist, your existing line forwards to it when it is busy or unanswered.

What happens when a call needs a real person?

The chat passes anything it cannot answer to you, with the visitor's details. The phone receptionist add-on can transfer the call to you, or take a message and flag the contact in the CRM.
