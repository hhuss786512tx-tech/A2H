The phone receptionist add-on, on a recorded test call

Hear it take a call, start to finish.

3:05 of real audio. Riley answers the phone for a sample mobile groomer, takes the caller's details and logs the call in the CRM, all while the groomer is out in the van. The receptionist is an add-on to an A2H website build.

The full callChapter 1 of 7 · Answers instantly

0:00 / 3:05

Riley, the AI receptionistCaller

About this recording. It is a real call between our receptionist and a test caller. The caller is another AI reading from a script we wrote, and Riley was never given that script. The business (Bluebonnet Mobile Grooming), the caller and the address are made up. The open times come from a sample schedule, not a live calendar. Everything else, the voice, the answers and the CRM record, is the actual system. The audio is not edited beyond levelling the volume and cutting it into the chapters below.
