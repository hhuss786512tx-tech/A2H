## The honest read on leverage

- **High leverage:** cold email + LinkedIn DM volume drive booked calls directly, and since the Loom pre-sells price and objections before anyone books, calls that land should close well above typical cold-outbound rates. This is where the $10k comes from.
- **High leverage, zero daily labor:** the new `pet-grooming.html` page on the site (see site commit) now runs 24/7 for search and AI-answer-engine traffic — mobile pet grooming was the one ICP with no page at all before this.
- **Lower leverage near-term:** 3 posts/day on LinkedIn and Instagram is a lot of content for organic reach that typically takes months to compound. It won't move a 97-day sprint much — consider cutting to ~1/day/platform and reallocating that time to personalizing DMs or working replies, which convert far faster.
- **Watch this:** every reply, DM and call currently routes through one person. At 500+ emails/day, reply volume will eventually outpace what one person can answer same-day.
