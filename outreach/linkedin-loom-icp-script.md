# LinkedIn ICP Outreach — Loom Video Script

**ICPs:** Mobile pet cleaning / mobile pet grooming owners, and dental clinic owners.

**Goal:** The Loom video does the entire sale — pain, solution, demo, objections, price, offer —
before the call ever happens. Anyone who books after watching the whole thing has already
decided. The call becomes onboarding, not convincing.

---

## The 3-step flow

1. **Connect** on LinkedIn with a short, personalized note (no link yet — links in a cold
   connection request tank acceptance rates).
2. **DM the Loom link** once they accept.
3. **The Loom pre-sells everything** — including price and objections — so the booked call is
   just setup.

---

**Pet cleaning / mobile grooming version:**

> for owners like you around missed calls and lead follow-up, would love to connect.

## Step 3 — The Loom script (record this)

Target length: **5–7 minutes**. This ICP is mobile/hands-on and busy — anything longer gets
abandoned. Show your face for the hook and the close; screen-share your pricing page / CRM
pipeline for the demo section.

### [0:00–0:30] Hook — call out their specific pain

**Pet cleaning:**
> "Hey, this is [Your Name] from A2H. I made this specifically for mobile pet cleaning and
> mobile grooming owners, so if that's not you, feel free to skip. If it is — I want to show
> you something that's probably costing you a few jobs a week without you even seeing it
> happen."

**Dental:**
> "Hey, this is [Your Name] from A2H. I made this specifically for dental practice owners, so
> if that's not you, feel free to skip. If it is — I want to show you something that's
> probably costing you a few new patients a month without you ever knowing it happened."

### [0:30–1:30] Agitate — the missed-call problem

> "Here's the thing: you're mobile, or you're mid-appointment, or your front desk is already
> on another line. When that happens, the call goes to voicemail. And here's what actually
> happens next — almost nobody leaves a voicemail anymore. They just call the next
> [groomer / dental practice] on Google. That's not a maybe, that's what people actually do.
> If you're missing even 4–5 calls a week and each one is worth [$150–350+ for a
> job / $200–2,000+ depending on the procedure], that's real money walking to a competitor
> every single week — and you don't even get a signal that it happened."

### [3:00–4:30] Pre-handle the objections — say them before they ask

> "A few things people usually ask me, so let me just get ahead of it:

>

---

## Execution notes

- Keep the video under 7 minutes — this ICP is busy and hands-on and will not watch a
  15-minute pitch.
- State price and answer objections **inside the video, not on the call**. Anyone who books
  after watching is pre-sold — treat the call as onboarding, not a pitch. Confirm details and
  get them scheduled; don't re-sell what they already agreed to.
- Swap the bracketed `[Name]`, `[Company]`, `[City]`, and dollar figures per prospect —
  generic mass-DMs read as spam and tank reply rates on LinkedIn.
